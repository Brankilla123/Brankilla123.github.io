Privacy Policy: Drive To Crush

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game Drive To Crush (the "Game") for Android collects and how it is used. The Game is developed by JaSTeRHuNt, an individual developer based in Latvia (the "Developer", "we").

1. Data we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (coins, cars, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your device.

2. Third-party services

The Game uses the following Google services. They may collect data directly from your device under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): shows ads. It may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, and may use cookies or similar technologies. How Google uses data from partner apps.
  • Google User Messaging Platform: asks for and stores your consent choices for ads where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states).
  • Google Play Games Services: optional sign-in, achievements and leaderboards. It uses your Play Games profile (player ID, nickname, avatar).
  • Google Play Billing: processes optional in-app purchases (for example, removing ads or a donation). Payments are handled by Google; we never see your card details.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: anonymous usage statistics (for example, which levels are played and crashes) to improve the Game. It may use the app instance ID and device information.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Why data is processed

  • To show ads, which keep the Game free (legal basis: your consent, or legitimate interest where permitted).
  • To measure ad performance and prevent fraud.
  • To provide sign-in, achievements and purchases you request.
  • To understand how the Game is used and fix problems.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Ad consent. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the Game asks for consent on first launch. You can change it at any time in Settings → Game → Ads and privacy.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset it or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Remove ads. An optional purchase turns off interstitial ads.
  • Delete data. Uninstalling the Game deletes all progress stored on your device. You can also reset progress in the Game settings.
  • Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object, and to complain to a supervisory authority (in Latvia: Datu valsts inspekcija, www.dvi.gov.lv). Because we do not store your data ourselves, most requests are handled by Google; we will help you with any request sent to the contact below.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and security

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google services is retained according to Google's policies. Data is transferred over encrypted connections.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
